There is a gap between two thoughts. Most of our lives, we leap across it so
quickly we never notice it is there. Meditation, at its simplest, is the slow
art of lingering in that gap.

## Noticing the gap

When you first sit, the mind feels like a crowded room. Thoughts arrive
uninvited, overlap, argue. The instinct is to silence them. But silence is not
the point; *spaciousness* is.

> You are not trying to stop the waves. You are learning to become the ocean.

After a while, something shifts. The thoughts don't stop, but the distance
between them widens. And in that widening, you catch a glimpse of the one who
has been watching all along.

## A small practice

1. Sit comfortably and let the eyes soften.
2. Follow one full breath, beginning to end.
3. At the top of the inhale, notice the tiny pause before the exhale.
4. Rest there, even for half a second.

That pause is the doorway. Keep returning to it. Nothing dramatic happens, and
that is exactly the point.
